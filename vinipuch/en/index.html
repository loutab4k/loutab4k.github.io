WHY VINIPUCH

01
Minimal product data

We keep only what the service needs: account details, payments, connection setup and total traffic used. No browsing history.

02
Transparent data policy

We publish what we process and how long it is kept — account, payments, access setup and traffic totals. Details are in the logging and retention policy.

03
Honest pricing

Top up a ruble balance via SBP, cryptocurrency or another available method. Plans are charged once from the balance. Auto-renew from balance is opt-in only — never from your card or wallet.

04
Connection quality

Speed depends on your network, device, route and server load. Try your usual sites, video and calls during a trial, if available.
